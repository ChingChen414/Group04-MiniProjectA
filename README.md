# Group 4 · Mini Project A

## Kipling Avenue and Dixon Road

Group 4’s final SMRTTECH 4SC3 showcase at McMaster University compares two fleet models and a revised signal split at Kipling Avenue and Dixon Road in Toronto. Open `index.html` locally or visit [Website A](https://chingchen414.github.io/Group04-MiniProjectA/). The companion [Website B](https://chingchen414.github.io/Group04-MiniProjectB/) presents the Oshawa GO station visualization.

## Contents

- `config.js` holds the project text, metrics and image captions.
- `assets/img/image1.jpg` through `image7.jpg` contain the project imagery and the two E61 speed–density plots.
- `assets/img/E61-signal-comparison.png` is the original baseline and signal-intervention figure from Group 4’s Report A.
- `assets/video/video.mp4` shows ten seconds each of HDV and CAV traffic at J1. Native SUMO-GUI frames show the submitted models at the original 0.2-second step. The CAV display colour is lightened for visibility.
- `assets/data/E61_speed_density.csv` contains the 36 common 100-second intervals for each of the three conditions. Density is the total across the four-lane edge.
