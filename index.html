Kipling Avenue and Dixon Road · Toronto

SUMO-Based Roadway Modelling and Speed–Density Assessment at Kipling Avenue and Dixon Road, Toronto

On the four-lane approach to the signal, nine more seconds of green raised mean interval speed by 40%. Changing the entire fleet to connected and autonomous vehicles changed that mean by 0.014 m/s.

Academic Demo

The same ten seconds at junction J1 play twice, first with yellow human-driven vehicles (HDVs), then with grey connected and autonomous vehicles (CAVs). Vehicle positions come from the two submitted SUMO models at simulation seconds 320–330.

        Study area

        Kipling Avenue crosses Dixon Road at junction J1 in Toronto. We traced the surrounding streets from the assigned GIS background, then selected the four-lane approach immediately east of the signal.

        Each lane on E61 is 43.42 m long and has a modelled limit of 13.89 m/s (50 km/h). All three scenarios sample this same approach.
